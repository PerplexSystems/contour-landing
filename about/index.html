About

We build the foundation before the answers.

Contour works with organisations that hold more data than their teams can interpret. Decades of transactions, years of instrumentation history, maintenance records sitting in systems that were never designed to speak to one another.

Everyone in your market is being sold the same thing this year: point an AI at the warehouse and ask it anything. The demonstration works. Production does not, because a model writing queries against a schema it does not understand is right most of the time, and the answers it gets wrong look exactly like the ones it gets right. A quietly incorrect number reaches a decision before anyone catches it, which is worse than getting no answer at all.

So we build the structured model first. Once your business is described properly, an assistant no longer has to guess at it. It calls operations that are defined, tested and versioned, and every answer can be checked against the records underneath it. That order of operations is the whole method, and it is why our engagements start with your systems rather than with a demonstration.

We work with clients across Europe, North America and Brazil.
