*Contour is a set of components that each do one job and share one model of your plant.* They are described separately because each answers a different question. In an engagement they are deployed together.

The components fall into two halves. The first describes the plant symbolically: what assets exist, what they are made of, which variable each sensor measures, and how assets depend on each other in series and in parallel. The second fits statistics and machine-learning models over that description, so every estimate knows which asset it is about and what fails with it.

Describe

* The symbolic layer: the plant written down as explicit structure.

#+begin_grid
#+begin_card
* Asset Tree

Site, area, system, equipment and component, with every node linked to its failures, work orders, sensors and documents.
#+end_card

#+begin_card
* Component Catalogue

Maps each make and model of equipment to its failure modes and to the variables that reveal them. A centrifugal pump arrives knowing that seal and bearing wear show up in vibration and temperature.
#+end_card

#+begin_card
* Model Context Layer

Maps every tag and field in your data onto the asset and the model variable it represents, with units, sampling rate and a confidence for each mapping.
#+end_card

#+begin_card
* [[/product/reliability-block-diagrams/][Reliability Block Diagrams]]

Describes how assets combine to deliver a function: in series, in parallel, k-out-of-n or on standby, including the elements redundant paths share.
#+end_card

#+begin_card
* Structure Rules

Declarative rules that derive the block diagrams from the asset tree and process topology, and update them when the plant changes.
#+end_card

#+begin_card
* Failure Propagation

Traces a component failure through the block diagrams to the function and the production it takes down, and shows what keeps running.
#+end_card
#+end_grid

* What the components make possible

* Not a rule engine

Structure Rules are declarative. They state what the structure of the plant is, for example that two pumps on one header with a duty and a standby designation form a one-out-of-two block, and Contour derives the block diagrams from them. Nothing fires. A rule engine holds conditions and actions and reacts when incoming facts match; Contour holds a description of the plant and answers questions asked of it.

That description is also what limits it. Contour answers only what the model describes, and a question that falls outside it comes back as a refusal rather than an inference.
