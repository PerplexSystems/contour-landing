Contour

A reasoning platform for the data you already have.

Contour turns your business into a structured model. You question it, plan against it and act on it directly. Every step traces back to the records it came from.

Connect the AI your team already uses. It reaches your business through Contour rather than through your databases, so every answer is built from verified operations instead of a query the model guessed at. The number still holds when someone asks where it came from.

Product

We offer an AI-native semantic system.

We already ship solutions for two industries: finance and manufacturing. Our modules handle the mapping between your data and the model, so most of that work is done before you begin.

Our platform ingests from multiple data sources and schematizes them into a semantic model tailored to the particulars of your business, then exposes a question endpoint so you can interact with it directly via an agentic. It runs under a closed world assumption: if something falls outside the model, it is not inferred. You get an assistant that tells you it does not know, rather than one that fills the gap with a plausible number.

Modules: Model context layer · Asset tree & failure propagation · Reliability engineering · Sensor intelligence
