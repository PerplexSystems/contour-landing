Product

One platform, four modules.

Contour turns the systems you already run into one governed model of your business, then puts that model in front of your analysts and the AI assistants they already work in. Four modules extend it into the areas where an answer has to understand physical equipment and not just tables.

Model Context Layer

Plug the AI assistant your team already uses straight into your business. It answers from verified operations rather than from a query it wrote itself, and every figure carries lineage back to source.

Asset Tree & Failure Propagation

See what a single failure takes down with it. Contour models the plant as a dependency graph rather than a list of tags, so consequence can be traced to the production it costs you.

Reliability Engineering

Push your maintenance window past the fortnight. Each asset's own failure history becomes a probability of failing inside the period you actually plan against.

Sensor Intelligence

Get an alarm that names a cause instead of a tag number. Contour ingests instrumentation continuously, collapses correlated alarms into one event and catches drift before it reaches your models.
